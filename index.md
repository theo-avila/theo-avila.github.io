My name is Theo Avila, and I am a PhD student in atmospheric chemistry at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. I am broadly interested in climate dynamics and climate impacts, and my current work focuses on aerosol–climate interactions. I am an NSF Graduate Research Fellow in Climate and Atmospheric Dynamics and a Dean of Science Fellow at MIT. Previously, I studied physical fire risk and the impacts of climate on agriculture while completing my undergraduate degree in physics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I also worked on physical oceanography and climate modeling at NOAA's Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory (GFDL) as a Lapenta and Hollings Scholar. This website provides a general overview of my work and interests. You can explore my [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and a selection of [papers and other works]({{ '/reading/' | relative_url }}) I have found insightful. Feel free to get in touch at [tcavila@mit.edu](mailto:tcavila@mit.edu).
